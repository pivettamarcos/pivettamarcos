PRION: a PICO-8 demake of an old classic videogame

10/05/2024 · 2 min read · tags: test, prion

project: PRION

Play the game

Now with online leaderboard! Log in with your newgrounds.com passport and beat other players' high scores.


Aliens are infecting Earth with a deadly disease. You have been tasked to destroy all spacecraft that pose a threat to our planet. Please act fast, as your reward is based on the amount of uninfected land in each wave of enemies. There are an estimated 10 waves of increasingly harder alien reinforcements. Reports also show that the extraterrestrials possess a device that can alter the force of gravity. We have equipped your ship with fuel and altitude gauges, a scanner, a laser cannon , and homing missiles.


You can land the ship at the base to fully refuel your craft. Additionally, you get one homing missile and a spare life every 5000 points. The following 3D scans were just sent to us by mission control and show the aspect of each alien spacecraft that is part of the invasion force:


We have been able to determine the behaviour of each enemy vessel, except for the last one, the mysterous spacecraft. Be extremely careful around it. 
Ship Behaviour
SeederA non-aggressive flying saucer that spreads the disease across the ground and plants
Drone An aggressive spacecraft that becomes stronger after it destroys a mutated plant
Bomber A non-aggressive carrier that drops infectious bombs that affects a very wide radius of the surroundings
Fighter The professional killer of the invasion force. It is an aggressive spacecraft designed to shoot any form of life that is against theirs
Pest A kamikazi-style alien spacecraft that follows and tries to crash into you
Attractor An aggressive spacecraft equipped with a high-tech tractor-beam that pulls any spacecraft in its vicinities
Mystery Spacecraft A secret new alien spaceship roumoured to have joined the invasion force,   and that may strike at any time. Beware, as it may have a  lethal new weapons system fitted.
Try and get a highscore of 140,338 or higher in those 10 waves, that means you're better than me. Good luck! The future of mankind is in your hands.

Controls

  1. Use arrow keys to orient the ship
  2. Press Z to thrust spacecraft
  3. Press X to shoot laser cannon
  4. Double-press X to shoot missile

Source Code and Graphics

The source code for the game can now be found on github. The 3D graphics were made with the freely available Zarchy Engine for pico-8. Go ahead and give them both a star to show support 😊
